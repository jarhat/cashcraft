YOUR FIRST STEPS

A simpler way to explore digital money

Bitcoin Cash is peer-to-peer electronic cash: you can send it directly to another wallet without relying on a payment intermediary. Its fixed supply is one reason supporters describe it as sound money.

CashTokens are digital tokens on Bitcoin Cash. To get started, connect a compatible wallet, check the token category and amount, and approve payments in your wallet. Keep your recovery phrase private and verify the details before sending.
