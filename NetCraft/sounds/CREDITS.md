# Game audio

- `forest-ambience.mp3`: “Forest Ambience” by TinyWorlds, CC0 1.0,
  https://opengameart.org/content/forest-ambience. The original MP3 is
  reproduced unchanged (SHA-256
  `9850aa1d0d5d66bd9c5daf8bb77c6d852e01f2f4de22f283bd5621e8bed13b75`).
- `step.wav`: gravel footstep from “Footsteps on different surfaces”,
  derived from Ali_6868's Gravel Footsteps, CC0,
  https://opengameart.org/content/footsteps-on-different-surfaces.
- `mine.wav` and `build.wav`: world impacts from Kenney “Impact Sounds”,
  CC0, https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds.

- `swim.wav` (`splash_02.ogg`) and `dive.wav` (`splash_04.ogg`): rubberduck,
  “40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX,” CC0 1.0,
  https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx.
  Retrieved from the `lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds` mirror at
  `f2b6264f9ab89fabc266914c3654685d68c5a39b` and converted to mono 22,050 Hz PCM WAV.

The gravel step and world impacts were obtained from the credited CC0 recordings in
`byteab/operation-ink` at commit `10c5857b05fca41b943f63c365c2f76cc7e80331`
and converted from AAC to mono 22,050 Hz PCM WAV.
